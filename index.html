Kiln dried or seasoned: what's the difference?

Our kiln dried birch hardwood is below 20% moisture for a clean, efficient burn. Our seasoned softwood is around 20%. Both are ready to burn on delivery.

Local firewood from Elgin since 2013

Moray Logs was set up in Elgin in April 2013. We supply quality hardwood and softwood logs and kindling at fair prices, delivered to your door.

Our kiln dried birch is below 20% moisture for a clean, efficient burn. Our seasoned logs sit at around 20%. Every log is cut between 20cm and 40cm to fit all types of fires and stoves.
